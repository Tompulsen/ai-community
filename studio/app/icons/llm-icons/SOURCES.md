# Model Icon Sources

The MiniMax, StepFun, Spark, Baichuan, Yi, Cohere, NVIDIA, Perplexity, and Z.ai
SVG assets are from Lobe Icons:
https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/tree/master/packages/static-svg/icons

Their MIT license is included in `LOBE-ICONS-LICENSE.txt`.

The Agnes AI bitmap in `public/model-icons/agnes.png` is the official site
brand mark from https://agnes-ai.com/images/biglogo.png, used to identify
Agnes AI models. Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners.
